BRIGHTPATH TECHNOLOGIES PVT. LTD.
Employee Handbook & HR Policy Manual
(This is a fictional company created for demonstration purposes only.)

SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY

1.1 Sick Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 12 sick leaves per calendar year. Sick leave is credited at the rate of 1 day per month and cannot be carried forward to the next year. Employees must inform their manager before 10:00 AM on the day of absence. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

1.2 Casual Leave
Employees receive 8 casual leaves per year, intended for personal matters requiring short notice. Casual leave cannot be combined with sick leave in the same request. A minimum of 1 day's notice is required, except in emergencies.

1.3 Annual/Earned Leave
Full-time employees earn 18 days of annual leave per year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days. Any leave beyond this cap will lapse at the end of the calendar year. Employees must apply for annual leave at least 5 working days in advance.

1.4 Maternity and Paternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in accordance with applicable law. Male employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

SECTION 2: WORK ARRANGEMENTS

2.1 Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval. Fully remote arrangements are evaluated on a case-by-case basis and require VP-level sign-off. Employees working remotely must be available on company communication tools during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST.

2.2 Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break. Flexible start times between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM are permitted, provided employees complete 8 working hours per day.

2.3 Overtime Policy
Non-managerial employees who work beyond 45 hours in a week are eligible for overtime compensation at 1.5x their standard hourly rate. Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager and logged in the timesheet system within 48 hours.

SECTION 3: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

3.1 Health Insurance
BrightPath Technologies provides comprehensive health insurance covering the employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children, with a coverage limit of ₹5,00,000 per year. Employees may opt for additional top-up coverage at their own cost through the HR portal.

3.2 Performance Bonus
Employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus ranging from 8% to 20% of their base salary, based on individual performance ratings and overall company performance. Bonus payouts occur in April of each year, following the annual review cycle.

3.3 Provident Fund
BrightPath Technologies contributes 12% of an employee's basic salary to the Employee Provident Fund (EPF) each month, matched by an equal employee contribution, in compliance with statutory requirements.

SECTION 4: WORKPLACE CONDUCT

4.1 Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, integrity, and respect in all workplace interactions. Harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in immediate termination.

4.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose proprietary company information, client data, or trade secrets to any external party during or after their employment, except as required by law.

4.3 Grievance Redressal
Employees may raise workplace grievances through the HR portal or directly with the HR Business Partner. All grievances will be acknowledged within 3 working days and resolved within 15 working days wherever possible.

SECTION 5: OFFBOARDING

5.1 Notice Period
The standard notice period for resignation is 60 days for employees at the manager level and above, and 30 days for all other employees. BrightPath Technologies may offer a buyout option for early release, subject to business requirements.

5.2 Exit Interview
All departing employees are required to complete an exit interview with HR prior to their last working day. Final settlement, including pending dues and reimbursements, will be processed within 45 days of the last working day.
